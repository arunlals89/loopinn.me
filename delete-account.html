Delete your Loopinn account

Last updated: 10 October 2026

You can delete your Loopinn account and its data at any time — in the app, or by emailing us if you no longer have the app. This page explains both ways and exactly what is deleted.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Loopinn and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the summary, type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (no app needed)

You don't need to reinstall or open the app. Send an email to hello@loopinn.me with the subject “Delete my Loopinn account”:

  • Write from the email address linked to your account, or tell us the phone number you signed up with.
  • If we can't be sure the request is from you, we may ask you to confirm a detail only the account owner would know.
  • We will delete the account and confirm by email, within 30 days at most — usually much sooner.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, date of birth, gender, bio, interests and what you are open to.
  • All your photos and thumbnails.
  • Your latest location point.
  • Your settings, blocks and notification tokens.
  • Your sign-in details (email, phone number, Apple or Google identifier).
  • The Loops you sent and received, and your conversations with their messages. Conversations are deleted for the other person too, so they will see you disappear.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Safety reports you made or that were made about you, with the link to your account removed — kept as long as needed for safety, to prevent repeat abuse, and for legal claims.
  • A minimal security record that an account was deleted (no name, photos, messages or contact details).
  • Backups: encrypted provider backups may still hold copies until they age out, within 30 days.
  • Anything we must keep to comply with the law.

See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Also worth doing

  • Sign in with Apple: to disconnect Loopinn from your Apple Account, go to Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, choose Loopinn, and tap Stop Using Apple Account.
  • Google sign-in: remove Loopinn at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Waitlist: if you only joined the website waitlist, email us and we'll delete your entry.
  • Just want a break? Turn on Ghost Mode (Me tab) to be invisible without deleting anything.
