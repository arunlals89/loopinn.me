Loopinn Support

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Need help with Loopinn? Start with the answers below, or email us — a real person reads every message.

Contact us

Email hello@loopinn.me. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your device model, operating system version and app version (Me tab → Version), and a screenshot if it helps. Never send your password or a sign-in code.

Safety first

If you are in danger, call your local emergency services (for example 112, 911 or 999). To report someone, open their profile or chat, tap the ⋯ button and choose Report or Block. Read the safety tips and our Child Safety Standards.

Frequently asked questions

Signing in

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder (for email) and that you entered your number with the country code, for example +44 7700 900123. Wait a minute, then tap Resend code. Codes expire after a short time, so use the newest one. Still stuck? Email us from the same address.

My date of birth is wrong

Your date of birth is locked after sign-up so that the 18+ check can't be bypassed. Email us from your account email with the correct date and we'll look at it.

Can I change my email or phone number?

Not yet inside the app. Email us from the address on your account and we'll help.

Nearby people and location

I can't see anyone nearby

Loopinn only shows people who are visible, within your radius and have the app open. Try a wider radius (up to 1 km), check that Location is allowed in your device settings, and make sure Ghost Mode is off. In quiet places there may simply be nobody around right now.

Why is the distance approximate?

To protect everyone, positions are blurred and distances are rounded to the nearest 50 m. Nobody can see your exact location. How it works.

How do I become invisible?

Turn on Ghost Mode on the Radar or in the Me tab. People nearby can no longer see you, but you can still look around.

Loops and chats

What is a Loop?

A Loop is a one-tap request to meet or hang out, with a vibe like coffee, a walk, dance or food. If the other person Loops you back it's a Loop — and chat opens. Chat never opens before that.

Why can't I send another Loop?

There is a daily limit, and after a Loop is declined or ignored there is a short cool-down before you can Loop the same person again.

I'm not getting notifications

Check notifications are allowed for Loopinn in your device settings, and that Loops and Messages are on under Me → Notifications. Muted chats don't notify.

Safety and your account

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile or your chat, tap the ⋯ button (in a chat: Report or block), and choose Report or Block. Blocking is instant and mutual. Manage blocks under Me → Blocked people. Reports are confidential. More details.

How do I delete my account?

Me tab → Delete account, or email us if you no longer have the app. See Delete your account.

How do I get a copy of my data, or correct it?

Edit most details under Me → Edit profile. For a copy of your data or other privacy requests, email us from your account email; see your rights.
