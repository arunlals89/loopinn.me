Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Loopinn is an app for adults. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) — including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and any sexualisation or endangerment of a minor — anywhere on Loopinn. This page sets out our published standards for the Loopinn app (Android package me.loopinn.app, and iOS).

Report a child-safety concern

  • In the app: open the profile or chat, tap the ⋯ button (in a chat: Report or block), tap Report, and choose Seems under 18, Safety concern or Inappropriate content.
  • By email: hello@loopinn.me with the subject “Child safety”.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

1. Our standards

The following are strictly prohibited on Loopinn, in profiles, photos, Loop notes, messages and any other content:

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including images, video, text or links;
  • grooming, enticing, soliciting or attempting to meet a minor for sexual or exploitative purposes;
  • sexualised content or comments involving a minor, including “sextortion”;
  • child trafficking or any other exploitation of children;
  • using Loopinn if you are under 18, or helping someone under 18 to use it.

2. How we prevent it

  • Adults only. Loopinn is limited to people aged 18 and over. We collect a date of birth at sign-up and the app refuses anyone under 18. The check is also enforced in our database, so it can't be skipped by modifying the app, and a date of birth can't be changed after sign-up.
  • Consent-based contact. People can only chat after both have accepted a Loop. There are no public feeds, no open group chats and no way to message a stranger directly.
  • Limited visibility. Profiles are only shown to people nearby, with approximate distance only. Photos are stored privately and only load for people allowed to see them.
  • Safety tools. Anyone can block or report any profile or chat from inside the app. Blocking is instant, and reporting someone is confidential.
  • Abuse limits. Rate limits restrict how many Loops and messages an account can send.

3. How we respond

  • We review child-safety reports as a priority, aiming to act within 24 hours.
  • We remove the content, suspend or permanently remove the accounts involved, and prevent them from returning where we can.
  • Where we have actual knowledge of CSAM, we remove it, preserve the relevant information as the law requires, and report it to the appropriate authority — in the United States, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, and elsewhere the competent national authority or hotline.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement investigations and valid legal requests.
  • We comply with applicable child-safety laws.

4. Where else to report

You can also report suspected child sexual abuse material directly to:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline (United States and international);
  • INHOPE — a network of hotlines in more than 50 countries;
  • Internet Watch Foundation (United Kingdom).

5. Child-safety contact

Loopinn's child-safety contact can discuss our CSAE prevention practices and our compliance with these standards: hello@loopinn.me (subject “Child safety”).
