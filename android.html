This is a beta

Some profiles you'll see are demo accounts that we use to test Loopinn. They are not real people near you. Features may change and things may break before launch. Everything else works like the real app: the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Community Guidelines all apply, you must be 18 or older, and you can delete your account at any time.

How to install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download the APK above and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back. You can turn it off again after installing.
  4. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may warn that it doesn't recognise the app, because it isn't from Google Play. Tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open Loopinn, sign in, and allow location when asked. That's what lets you see who's nearby.

Updates

Small fixes arrive automatically. When you open Loopinn it checks for an update in the background. If there is one, it applies the next time you fully close and reopen the app. For bigger updates we'll post a new version here: download it and install it over the old one. Your account and settings are kept.

Uninstalling

Go to Settings → Apps → Loopinn → Uninstall. Removing the app does not delete your account. To do that, follow Delete your account. Trouble installing? See Support or email hello@loopinn.me.
