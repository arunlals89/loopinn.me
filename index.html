    Privacy & safety

    Meeting strangers should feel exciting, not risky.

    Safety isn't a settings page here — it's how Loopinn is built.

    You choose who sees you

    Public, or invisible. Switch Ghost Mode on in one tap, and set quiet zones like home and work.

    Never your exact spot

    People see an approximate distance and a softened pin — never an address or a precise location.

    Mutual by design

    No unsolicited messages. A chat only opens after both people accept a Loop.

    Verified & 18+

    Optional selfie verification earns a badge. Loopinn is strictly for adults.

    Share your Loop

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    What exactly is a “Loop”?

    A Loop is your one-tap invitation to meet or hang out — coffee, a walk, a dance, whatever you're in the mood for. The other person gets a notification. If they Loop you back, it's a match and chat opens.

    Is Loopinn a dating app?

    It's built for real-life connection. You choose what you're open to — dating, new friends or a spontaneous hangout — and it shows on your profile.

    Can people see exactly where I am?

    No. Other people only see an approximate distance and a softened pin. Your precise location is never shown, and you can go invisible with Ghost Mode at any time.

    How big is the radius?

    You decide, from roughly 100 m up to 1 km. Smaller for a packed venue, larger for a quiet neighborhood.

    How do I sign up?

    With Google, Apple or your mobile number. Then a short onboarding to add photos, a bio and what you're looking for.

    When does it launch, and what does it cost?

    An Android beta is out now. iOS is on the way. Pricing isn't final yet; the beta and the waitlist are free and commit you to nothing.

    Can I delete my account and my data?

    Yes, any time. In the app go to Me, then Delete account — your profile, photos, Loops and chats are erased straight away. You can also request deletion by email. Details are on our account deletion page.

    Who can use Loopinn?

    Adults only (18+). We verify, moderate and make it easy to block or report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
