Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Loopinn helps you meet people nearby. To do that we handle personal information — including your location and photos — so this page explains exactly what we collect, why, who can see it, and how you stay in control.

The short version

  • Adults only. You must be 18 or older.
  • Location only while the app is open. We never collect location in the background.
  • Nobody sees where you are. Other people see a rounded distance (to the nearest 50 m) from a deliberately blurred position — never your exact location.
  • We keep one location point, briefly. Only your latest point is stored, it counts as expired after 10 minutes, and it is deleted automatically.
  • No selling, no ad tracking. We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • Chat is mutual. A conversation only opens after both people accept a Loop.
  • You are in control. Edit your profile, go invisible with Ghost Mode, block and report, or delete your account — all inside the app.

4. What other people can see

If you are visible (not in Ghost Mode) and not blocked, people within the search radius of your approximate position can see your first name, age (unless you turn off “Show my age”), photos, bio, interests, what you are open to, an approximate distance, and a verified badge when that feature is available.

  • Never shown to others: your email address, phone number, date of birth, or exact location.
  • Blurred position: we snap your position to a grid of roughly 110 m and shift it slightly — differently for each viewer — before measuring distance, which is then rounded to 50 m. Changing the search radius cannot be used to pinpoint you.
  • Photos are private by default. Your photos are stored in a private bucket and only load for people who can currently see you or who have an open Loop or chat with you, through short-lived links.
  • Loops and chats are visible to the person you are interacting with. A chat only opens after both of you accept a Loop. Anyone you chat with could copy or screenshot what you share, and we can't prevent that.
  • Ghost Mode hides you from everyone instantly. The app still uses your location while you browse so you can look around, but nobody can see you.

A limit worth knowing: distance blurring protects you in crowds, but if you are the only person within a few hundred metres, anyone who sees you on their Radar knows someone is nearby. Use Ghost Mode whenever you want to be invisible.

6. Location in detail

  • Foreground only. The app reads your location only while it is open and showing the Radar. It stops when you leave the app.
  • Light touch. While the Radar is open, your device sends a fresh point roughly every 12 to 20 seconds, and only when you move or the previous point is getting old.
  • One point, no history. Our servers keep only your latest point. It counts as expired after 10 minutes without an update, a clean-up job deletes points older than 2 hours, and it is deleted when you delete your account. We don't build a location history.
  • Blurred before anyone sees it — see section 4.
  • Never in logs or analytics. Coordinates are not written to our logs, analytics events or notifications.
  • Your controls. Turn on Ghost Mode, switch off location permission in your device settings, or simply close the app.

8. Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Edit your name, photos, bio, interests and what you are open to (Me → Edit profile).
  • Control visibility with Ghost Mode and “Show my age”.
  • Control notifications for Loops and messages.
  • Block or report anyone, and manage the people you have blocked.
  • Delete your account at any time: Me → Delete account. See how account deletion works.

Your date of birth is locked after sign-up so the 18+ check can't be bypassed. If it is wrong, contact support.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to certain uses, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier use). You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

To use a right, email hello@loopinn.me from the email address or phone number linked to your account. We may need to verify it is you. We respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires, and don't charge for reasonable requests. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

9. Children

Loopinn is for adults only. You must be at least 18. The app checks your date of birth at sign-up and our database refuses any birth date that makes someone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18 we delete it. If you believe someone is under 18, use Report → Seems under 18 in the app or email us. See our Child Safety Standards.

10. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Access rules are enforced in the database itself (row-level security), not just in the app, so people can only read what they are allowed to see.
  • Other people's location, birth date, email and phone number are never returned to the app.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in encrypted storage on your device, protected by the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.
  • Rate limits, an age gate and reporting tools help prevent abuse. Only authorised team members can access production data.

No service is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please email hello@loopinn.me. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

11. International transfers

Loopinn and our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where the law requires, we use safeguards for those transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK addendum, or an adequacy decision.

12. Website, waitlist and cookies

  • No cookies, no trackers. This website sets no cookies, uses no advertising or analytics scripts, and serves its fonts from its own server. The interactive radar demo runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to us.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address and request details under its own privacy statement.
  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist we store your email address, your city if you choose to enter it, which form you used and when. It is kept in our database (insert-only for the public; only the Loopinn team can read it) and used only to tell you about Loopinn's launch. Ask us to delete it at any time.
