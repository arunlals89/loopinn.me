Privacy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Draft. This covers the pre-launch waitlist only. A full privacy policy (location, photos, messages, retention, your rights) must be written and reviewed before the app launches.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist.
  • Your city, only if you choose to enter it.
  • Which form you used (for example “hero” or “footer”) and the time you signed up.

Why

Only to tell you about Loopinn — when it launches and where. We don't sell your data and we don't send anything unrelated.

Where it's stored

In our Supabase database. Access is restricted to the Loopinn team.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your waitlist entry at any time by emailing hello@loopinn.me.
